# Pong

A local, single-player recreation of the classic arcade paddle game.

## Language

**Paddle**: A vertical rectangle controlled by a player or the CPU.
_Avoid_: bat

**Ball**: The square game object exchanged between paddles.
_Avoid_: puck

**Rally**: Continuous play between serves.
_Avoid_: round

**Serve**: The launch of the ball from the center after a reset.
_Avoid_: kickoff

**Point**: A unit of score awarded when the opponent misses the ball.
_Avoid_: goal
