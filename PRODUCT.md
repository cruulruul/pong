# Product

## Platform

Web, opened as a local file in a desktop browser.

## Stack

One standalone HTML document with inline CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No external dependencies, assets, framework, build, or deployment.

## Users

A desktop keyboard user who wants to play a quick classic arcade game locally.

## Product purpose

Recreate classic human-versus-CPU Pong with no setup, external dependencies, or hosting. Success is completing a playable first-to-ten match.

## Operating context

Open `pong.html` directly from disk. Up and Down control the left paddle; Space starts or restarts; P pauses or resumes.

## Capabilities and constraints

Square responsive field, beatable CPU, physics-based rallies, scored matches, one-second serves toward the player who conceded, silent operation, no mobile controls, no persistence.

## Brand commitments

1970s monochrome Pong identity, pure black and solid white rectangular graphics.

## Product principles

- Immediate local play.
- Minimal native-browser implementation.
- Fair, readable, predictable gameplay.
